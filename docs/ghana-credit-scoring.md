# Ghana credit bureau factors and scoring models

Ghana does not have one national score. The Bank of Ghana licenses three bureaus, and only one of them publishes the weights in its model.

| Bureau | Licensed | What it sells | Score model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| XDS Data Ghana | First bureau; full licence from 2010 | The report most lenders pull. XDS says about 90% of lenders use it. | Score capacity has been claimed on a 300–850 scale. The Bank of Ghana’s 2024 report does not name XDS as one of the bureaus that launched a new scoring product. Weights are not public. |
| Dun & Bradstreet Credit Bureau Ghana | Since 2008; licensed 2010 | Consumer and commercial reports, plus monitoring. | The Bank of Ghana says D&B introduced a credit score in 2024–25. Weights are not public. Its older public description matches a classic file score, not the myCreditScore formula. |
| myCredit Score | Licence No. 004, October 2024 | A consumer score with a published formula. | The only model with stated weights. |

The Bank of Ghana approved bureau scoring in the 2024 credit-reporting year after reviewing data quality and methodologies, and said it will keep checking those models for fairness. In 2024, institutions ran 29.5 million searches, up from 13.7 million. 76 percent returned a file.

## Classic file score (XDS and D&B, as publicly described)

Built from the credit file lenders already submit:

- Repayment history (the largest piece)
- Amount owed
- Length of the credit history
- New applications and enquiries

XDS’s consumer report is the raw file: identity, facilities, public records such as judgments, and who enquired. A 2015 D&B Ghana interview listed payment history, current debt, length, mix of credit, and how often someone applies. No Ghana weights were published.

## myCreditScore’s published formula

This is not a FICO-style model. Cheques, court judgments, income, and identity are scored directly.

| Factor | Weight | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credit profile and repayment history | 60% | On-time payments and balances kept clear |
| Dishonoured cheques | 15% | Returned cheques and blacklist status |
| Judgment debt | 10% | Unpaid court debts |
| Socio-economic | 7% | Job, income, living situation |
| Economic assets | 5% | Bank balance, as a cushion |
| Demographics | 3% | Age and nationality, as identity |

myCreditScore says a score above 550 improves the chance of approval. They do not publish Excellent, Good, or Fair cut-offs. The 721 / 661 / 601 bands in this app are US-style labels, not a published Ghana scale.

Separately, myCreditScore markets a lender default model. That one uses age, occupation, location, marital status, and digital behaviour. It is not the six-factor consumer score. The Bank of Ghana’s job is to watch that kind of methodology so a score is not just a proxy for who someone is.

## What this app does

The six factors and the 60 / 15 / 10 / 7 / 5 / 3 split match myCreditScore only. Showing those same weights on the XDS and D&B tiles overstates what those bureaus publish.
